Symbolic Encodings of One Grammar

A viewer for one pre-agreed grammar of 294,912 sentences. The 273-character sentence below is one state of it, and every figure on this page is for that one sentence. This is not general text compression.

What these numbers do and do not mean Read first
  • The frame and meta-layer results hold for one pre-agreed grammar of 294,912 sentences (18.17 bits). Sender and receiver must hold the same codebook. Text outside the grammar cannot be encoded by these layers; the token-aware router sends it Direct (0% saving).
  • Character reduction is not token reduction. The one-code-point Layer 12 glyph costs 4 cl100k tokens, not 1, and a model without the codebook cannot read !!!.
  • The generic lossless codecs (zlib+runes, zlib+base85) cost more LLM tokens than the raw text: 1.8-3.3x for runes and 0.9-1.6x for Base85 (README, first 6,000 characters of three files, cl100k and o200k). Use them for storage or transport, not prompts.
  • Tiers 1-3 are lossy abbreviations with no decoder guarantee. Tier 6 and Layers 7-12 are exact only for states of the grammar.
  • Token counts in the tables are static, measured offline with tiktoken (benchmark_tokens.py). Characters and bytes are computed in this page. No model was run, and there is no speed claim.
